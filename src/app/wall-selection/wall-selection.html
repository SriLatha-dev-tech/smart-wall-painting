Select Wall Area

Click around the wall to create a selection.

Selection Tools

Click points around the wall. Select at least 3 points.

How to select a wall

  1. Click on the first corner of the wall.
  2. Click on the second corner.
  3. Continue clicking around the wall.
  4. If you make a mistake, click Undo Last Point.
  5. Select at least three points.
  6. Click Finish Selection.
  7. Click Continue → to choose a colour.
